World Tour

A classic-style platforming adventure across 5 kingdoms — 20 levels, tons of enemies, power-ups, and a castle boss at the end of every world.

Controls

⬅➡ or A/D — Move
⬆ / Space / W — Jump (hold longer = jump higher)
Shift — Run
⬇ / S — Crouch / Enter pipe
Stomp enemies from above to defeat them. Grab power-ups for special abilities.

Power-ups:
🍄 Mushroom — grow big, survive one extra hit
🔥 Fire Flower — throw fireballs
⭐ Star — brief invincibility
🪶 Feather — glide/slow-fall
🛡️ Shield — absorbs one hit

On mobile: use the on-screen buttons.
