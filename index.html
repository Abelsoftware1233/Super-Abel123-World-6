🥕 SUPER ABEL 123 WORLD 🥕

Abel can't stomp — but he can pull veggies, rocks and bombs right out of the ground and throw them at anything in his way. 20 levels, 5 worlds, a boss at the end of each!

Controls

⬅➡ or A/D — Move
⬆ / Space / W — Jump
⬇ / S — Duck
E / Tap grab button — Pull something out of a glowing patch, or pick up a stunned enemy
E again (while carrying) — Throw whatever you're holding

Abel doesn't defeat enemies by jumping on them — jumping on most enemies just bounces him off! Instead: pull an item from a glowing dirt patch and throw it at an enemy to stun or defeat it. Stunned enemies can be picked up and thrown too, like living projectiles.

Pull-ups:
🥕 Carrot — basic throw, stuns most enemies
🪨 Rock — heavy, breaks armored enemies and cracked blocks
💣 Bomb — explodes shortly after landing, clears a whole group
⭐ Golden Turnip — brief invincibility while carried
🧊 Ice Chunk — freezes an enemy solid on impact

On mobile: use the on-screen buttons.
